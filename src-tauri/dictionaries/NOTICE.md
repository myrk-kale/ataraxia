# Word lists

The `*.txt` files here are the 20,000 most frequent words of each language, with their counts,
taken from [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave
(built from OpenSubtitles 2018). The content is licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); the lists were filtered to whole
words and cut to the top 20,000 by `scripts/build-dictionaries.mjs`. They stay under that
license, separate from the MIT-licensed code.
